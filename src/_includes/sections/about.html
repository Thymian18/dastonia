The band

Dastonia makes heavy music with room to breathe. Crushing guitars and powerful drums meet atmospheric, spherical textures, and fragile, emotional passages sit right up against the weight. Modern elements run through every song, keeping the sound open and unpredictable.

Based in Bern, the band is writing and producing its first songs and preparing its debut live set for early 2027.
