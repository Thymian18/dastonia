01 — Bio

Dastonia is a Swiss metalcore band blending spherical, atmospheric textures with heavy guitars and powerful drums, balanced by emotional, fragile passages and modern, hip-hop-inspired elements. The sound sits close to acts like Future Palace, with influences including Spiritbox, Currents, and Architects.

Dastonia is a metalcore band based in Bern, Switzerland. The lineup came together through the FindMyBand platform, with the singer and drummer connecting in December 2025 and the full band completed after auditions in January 2026. Musically, Dastonia combines heavy, guitar-driven metalcore with spherical, atmospheric textures and emotional, fragile passages, woven together with modern elements such as hip-hop-inspired sections. The style sits close to bands like Future Palace, drawing further inspiration from artists such as Spiritbox, Currents, and Architects. The band is currently writing and producing its first songs, working toward five to six stage-ready tracks by the end of 2026 and a first run of live shows starting in 2027.
