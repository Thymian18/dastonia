The band

Dastonia started with a message on FindMyBand in December 2025, when the singer and drummer found they wanted to build the same kind of record: heavy, but not only heavy. Auditions the following month completed the lineup.

The four members grew up scattered across Switzerland, in Grisons, Lucerne, and Bern, and now all live in Bern, where the band writes and rehearses. The sound pulls together atmospheric, spherical textures with heavy guitars and powerful drums, then lets emotional, fragile parts sit right up against modern hip-hop-inspired elements.

The band is currently writing and producing its first songs, working toward five to six stage-ready tracks and a first run of live shows starting in early 2027.
